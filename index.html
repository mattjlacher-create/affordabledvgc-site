Warning signs

How Do You Know You Need a Dryer Vent Cleaning?

  • Is it taking longer than 35 minutes to dry a load of laundry?
  • Are you turning your dryer on multiple times to finish drying a load of laundry?
  • Do you smell a burning smell?
  • Is your dryer not heating up, drying your clothes to completion or shutting off mid cycle?
  • Is your dryer hot to the touch when it’s running?
  • Do you smell a mildew smell when removing your clothes from the dryer?
  • Is your lint trap collecting more lint than usual?
  • Is your electric or gas bill higher than normal?

If the answer is yes to even one of these, don't ignore the issue. It probably means your dryer is clogged with debris.

Not only does this waste energy and cause your dryer to work harder than usual, but this can also lead to a fire. You'll need an experienced professional exhaust technician to remedy the issue, and that's where we come in.

Bird Nest Removal & Prevention

We also Specialize in Birds Nest Removal and Prevention from Dryer Vents and Bathroom Exhaust Vents

Birds are always looking for a warm place to build a nest. Since dryer vents and bathroom exhaust vents are convenient for most passerine birds due to their small size and capacity to release heat, it's common to find a bird who's decided to “move in”.

How often should my dryer vent be cleaned?

Lint and debris can build up in your dryer vent duct and dryer hose. This reduces the air flow and causes a backup of lint, which is highly flammable. You can avoid these hazards by having your dryer and its ventilation system professionally inspected and cleaned every year.

Do you clean the inside of the dryer?

Yes. Most dryer fires start inside the dryer, so we disassemble and clean the inside of the dryer as part of our service. It’s called Dryer De‑Linting, and we’re one of the only companies in the area that includes it.

Why should I clean my dryer vents?

Fire prevention, improved dryer efficiency, mold prevention, asthma and allergy control, dust elimination and fresh air control. Clogged vents cause dryers to run hotter and work harder and longer to dry clothes.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Cleaning out gutters in the spring and fall might seem like a menial task, but failure to do so can cause major problems for a home. From foundation damage to roof leaks, clogged gutters can lead to expensive repairs when left untreated.

Do you install new gutters and gutter guards?

Yes. We offer KWM Iron Man Seamless 5″ & 6″ K-Style gutters, and we install, repair and sell gutter guards. We can install and clean any style gutter, any material, any color, so don’t forget to ask about our “dual services” discount.
