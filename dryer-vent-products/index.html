Low-Profile Exhaust Covers

Ask Us About Low-Profile Dryer Vent Exhaust Covers

DryerWallVents manufactures an attractive exhaust cover that lies nearly flat on the exterior wall of your home, hotel, or laundromat.

Made with a gravity-assist damper and integrated magnets, the cover is constructed of powder-coated galvanized steel and comes in a range of colors designed to complement most home and building exteriors.

Hoods & Cages

Keep Birds, Rodents, and Other Pests Out of Your Vents

Protect yourself and innocent creatures by letting us install a long-lasting Defender hood and cage at the terminal point of your dryer vent.

Teeth and beaks that can tear through screens and plastic will meet their match with our zinc-coated steel cage. Its vertical bars are designed to minimize lint buildup, and the slanted top provides additional weather protection.

Several colors are offered, and the cages work on all kinds of siding.

Products We Install

Our Dryer Vent Products

Magnetic Wall Vent Hood

This will be the last Hood you ever have to install. It’s a heavy-duty, galvanized steel hood, powder coated & painted, allows 100% airflow for more efficiency, prevents lint build up & faster dry times. The best part about this product is that it has magnets built into it so that birds or any others pests can’t get in and build nests.

Defender Cage

Heavy duty galvanized steel, powder coated & painted for long lasting protections from birds and rodents. Many people make the mistake by installing the plastic cages or mesh screens. However, the cages and screens aren’t code due to the lint not being able to escape, making it a fire hazard or burn your dryer out.

Dryer Roof Jack

This is a heavy-duty roof jack for your dryer vent roof termination. We install these along with all of our other products precisely to insure that there are no leaks.

Industrial Dryer Roof Jack

For salons, nail salons, groomers or any other type of commercial use for a dryer vent termination.

Dryer Flex

Transition duct for behind the dryer, it’s fire resistant up to 3 minutes, more energetic and efficient, and doesn’t kink or crush. The ends are taped with UL-Listed metal tape to ensure no fraying for long-lasting flexible ductwork. They are installed with stainless steel hose clamps.

Dryer ELL

Extended elbows for more efficiency, faster dry times, prevents lint build up and makes annual cleanings a little easier.

Dryer Box

The recessed dryer box gives you those extra 4″ you need in your laundry room to push your dryer back further to the wall.

Washer Supply Lines

Did you know that you’re supposed to replace your washer supply lines every 5 years? The rubber ones should especially be replaced, they dry rot and can break, causing a flood. Ask us about our braided stainless steel supply lines.
