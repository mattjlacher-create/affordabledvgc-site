Ask Us About Low-Profile Dryer Vent Exhaust Covers

DryerWallVents manufactures an attractive exhaust cover that lies nearly flat on the exterior wall of your home, hotel, or laundromat.

Made with a gravity-assist damper and integrated magnets, the cover is constructed of powder-coated galvanized steel and comes in a range of colors designed to complement most home and building exteriors.
