Keep Birds, Rodents, and Other Pests Out of Your Vents

Protect yourself and innocent creatures by letting us install a long-lasting Defender hood and cage at the terminal point of your dryer vent.




Teeth and beaks that can tear through screens and plastic will meet their match with our zinc-coated steel cage. Its vertical bars are designed to minimize lint buildup, and the slanted top provides additional weather protection.




Several colors are offered, and the cages work on all kinds of siding.
